DAF (Dissolved Air Flotation) Systems

  • Highly effective for the removal of fats, oils, and grease (FOG) from industrial effluents
  • Ideal for industries generating oil and grease heavy effluents, such as food processing and automotive units

  • A proven, eco-friendly treatment solution that reduces reliance on harsh chemicals

  • Delivers high treatment efficiency with minimal chemical dependency and reduced sludge generation

How Our High Effective Effluent Treatment Plant Works

A sequential, multi-stage treatment methodology engineered for maximum contaminant removal and safe water recovery.

01
Pre-treatment
  • Screening chamber for coarse solids
  • Oil & grease trap
  • Equalization tank with air agitation
02
Primary Treatment
  • Coagulation and flocculation using alum / ferric chloride
  • Clarifier or tube settler for sedimentation
03
Secondary Treatment
  • Biological oxidation via SBR, MBBR, or ASP process
  • Aeration tank with diffusers and blowers
  • Secondary clarifier for biomass separation
04
Tertiary Treatment
  • Pressure sand filter (PSF) and activated carbon filter (ACF)
  • UV or chlorine disinfection
  • Optional RO system for reuse
05
Sludge Handling
  • Sludge thickener, filter press, or drying beds
  • Safe disposal as per Hazardous Waste Rules
Our Process

What We Do Before Starting ETP Manufacturing

With over 15 years of experience, Ikon Smart Solutions is a trusted company in the design, manufacture, and installation of advanced effluent treatment systems across India. We follow the steps below before starting manufacturing your ETP project.

1
Assess Effluent Characteristics

We begin by analyzing the wastewater generated by your industry and testing key parameters like BOD, COD, TSS, pH, and oil & grease content. This helps us understand exactly what needs to be treated before designing the system.

2
Calculate Daily Effluent Generation

Based on your production capacity, water usage, and process cycles, we estimate the total volume of wastewater generated daily. For example, a mid-sized textile dyeing unit may generate several thousand litres (KLD) of effluent requiring treatment.

3
Apply a Safety (Peak) Factor

Industrial effluent flow can vary with production schedules and peak operating hours. To ensure uninterrupted treatment performance, we design plants with a safety margin of 1.25x to 1.5x the average daily flow.

4
Select the Right ETP Technology

Depending on the effluent composition and industry type, we choose the most suitable wastewater treatment process — whether that's DAF, enzymatic treatment, ZLD, or a combination of technologies — to achieve the required outlet standards efficiently.

5
Verify Compliance & Get Expert Guidance

We check mandatory discharge norms set by the State Pollution Control Board (PCB) and local environmental clearance requirements, since permissible limits vary by industry and location. For larger or complex setups requiring MLD-scale capacity, our team ensures your plant is accurately sized, fully compliant, and built for future expansion.

An Effluent Treatment Plant is built using several key components, each performing a specific role in the treatment process:

  • Bar Screen (SS304) — removes large solids from incoming effluent
  • Equalization Tank (RCC/FRP) — balances flow and pollutant load for consistent processing
  • Aeration System (SS diffusers and blowers) — supplies oxygen to support biological treatment
  • Clarifier (MS/RCC) — settles suspended solids, separating clear water from sludge
  • Filters (PSF/ACF, FRP or SS) — remove fine particles and odor for improved water quality
  • Dosing System (FRP/SS) — adds chemicals for pH correction and coagulation
  • Sludge Handling Units (MS/FRP) — manage dewatering and safe disposal of sludge generated during treatment
