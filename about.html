Our Profile

Ikon Smart Solutions

Ikon Smart Solutions established with Vision such as, to provide end-to-end solutions right from the stage of concept to execution in the field of Environmental Engineering.

We are the environmental engineering company, offering solution for water & wastewater treatment as well as supply a wide range of tailor made pollution control equipment’s to suit client’s needs for compliance to the most stringent effluent discharge norms. We are strongly involved in all industries where water is an important resource, utility or by product.

Our services cover the field from consultancy & engineering to turnkey project implementation as well as various equipment manufacturing & supply. If desired we will be also delighted to take care of your operation and maintenance with respect to water & wastewater treatment plants. We understand the importance of water, so we also undertake water audits which help industries to manage their water requirements and controlling pollution.

Quality Policy

We at Ikon Smart Solutions Projects Consultant are involved in providing quality technology solutions, supply, implementation and after sales services for Water, Wastewater & Effluent Treatment Plant. Also committed to comply with Quality Management System requirements with continuously improvising it’s effectiveness; delivering timely & cost effective solutions/services to meet the expectation of our customer vis-à-vis satisfying applicable statutory & regulatory requirements.
