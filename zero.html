Process Workflow

How Does the Ikon Smart Solutions ZLD Work?

Our zero liquid discharge plant treats industrial wastewater in stages so that almost all of it is recovered as reusable water and the leftover salts are converted into dry solid waste. No liquid effluent leaves the factory.

1
Pre-treatment (ETP Stage)

The process starts with pre-treatment in an effluent treatment plant (ETP), which removes suspended solids, oils, COD, BOD, and other contaminants through physical, chemical, and biological treatment. This prepares the wastewater for advanced treatment and protects membranes and evaporators from fouling and scaling.

2
High-Recovery Reverse Osmosis (RO)

The pre-treated water passes through a high-recovery RO system, which separates purified water from dissolved salts. The recovered water can be reused in processes, cooling towers, or utilities, while the salts remain in a smaller volume of concentrated reject water.

3
Brine Concentration

Next, the RO reject goes into a brine concentrator, which raises the salt concentration further and recovers more water. This reduces the load on the evaporator and keeps energy consumption low.

4
Evaporation & Crystallization

The concentrated brine is heated in evaporation units, such as multiple effect evaporators (MEE) or mechanical vapor recompression (MVR) systems. The water evaporates and is condensed for reuse, while the crystallizer turns the remaining brine into solid salt crystals.

5
Drying & Solid Residue

Finally, the remaining slurry is dried in an agitated thin film dryer (ATFD), leaving only dry solid residue for disposal or salt recovery. With automated PLC-SCADA monitoring and energy-efficient operation, the plant achieves complete zero liquid discharge.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs About ZLD Plants in India

A ZLD plant is an industrial wastewater treatment system that recovers almost all wastewater as reusable water and converts the leftover dissolved salts into solid waste. No liquid effluent is discharged outside the factory. It combines effluent treatment, reverse osmosis, evaporation, crystallization, and drying.

ZLD is commonly required in water-intensive and high-pollution industries such as textile and dyeing, chemical, pharmaceutical, distillery, food and beverage, paper, and power. The requirement depends on the state pollution control board and CPCB norms for your industry and location, so it is best to check with your local board before planning a plant.

A well-designed ZLD plant typically recovers 90 to 95 percent or more of the wastewater as reusable water, which can go back into processes, cooling towers, or utilities. The exact recovery depends on the wastewater characteristics, TDS level, and the treatment technologies used.

The cost varies widely with plant capacity, wastewater quality, industry type, the technologies used (MEE, MVR, ATFD, RO), and the level of automation. A detailed wastewater analysis is needed for an accurate quotation, so most manufacturers design and price each plant to the client's requirements.

Look for a manufacturer with proven installations in your industry, in-house design, manufacturing, and commissioning capability, and quality certifications such as ISO. Also check for PLC-SCADA automation, energy-efficient technology, and reliable after-sales service and technical support. Site visits or references from existing clients help confirm performance claims.
