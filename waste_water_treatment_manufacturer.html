Selecting the right wastewater treatment system depends on several factors, including wastewater characteristics, daily flow rate, industry type, COD, BOD, TSS, oil and grease, colour, chemical composition, discharge requirements, and water reuse objectives. A detailed wastewater analysis helps determine the most suitable treatment process and plant configuration. Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) is an advanced industrial wastewater treatment approach designed to minimize or eliminate liquid wastewater discharge from a facility. ZLD systems typically recover and reuse treated water while concentrating and managing the remaining dissolved solids through suitable treatment and evaporation processes. ZLD is commonly considered where water reuse and stringent liquid discharge requirements are important.
