Advanced Wastewater Treatment System

Key Features of Smart Sewage Treatment Plant (STP)

Advanced SBR Technology

SUses Sequenced Batch Reactor (SBR) process combines aeration and clarification in a single tank for efficient treatment

Fully Automated Operation

Centralized smart controller manages the entire treatment cycle, reducing dependency on manual monitoring

Airlift Technologye

Replaces mechanical pumps for water transfer, sludge removal, and decantation which helps to minimize equipment failure and maintenance needs.

High Treatment Efficiency

Achieves treated water quality of BOD < 10 mg/l and COD < 50 mg/l

Smart Alarm System

Digital monitoring detects abnormal functions and triggers alerts for proactive maintenance

Scalable Capacity

Modular STP design supports a wide capacity range from 1 KLD to 10 MLD

Energy Efficient

Smart Power Save mode adjusts operation based on tank filling levels, optimizing energy use

Our wastewater treatment systems are available in capacities ranging from 1 KLD to 100 MLD.
